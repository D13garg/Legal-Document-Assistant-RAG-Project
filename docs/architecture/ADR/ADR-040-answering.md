# ADR-040: Answering

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Approval state (2026-10-18): the design decisions **Q1-Q4 below were approved by the sole project owner** in the Step 16 conversation, including the narrow, additive `grounded_generation.api` and `api.lock` change (Q1). **The project owner accepted Step 16 and this ADR on 2026-10-18**, based on the delivered implementation, the documented decisions, the validation report and the verified archive (SHA-256 `a66a5fb150d8f17459fb8f44a5af4797c7fd53d413e75e4a8ae4d70cb0e635a3`). Implementation and passing tests remain separate facts from acceptance, and acceptance does not remove any limitation listed in this ADR. These are project-owner approvals only (a conversation record), not a team sign-off or a code-owner review; `CODEOWNERS` is unchanged and does not route the module (as in Steps 14 and 15).

The approved DAG gives `answering` six edges: `retrieval`, `grounded_generation`, `conversations`, `access_control`, `ai_gateway`, `prompts`. The DAG file is unchanged. It has no edge to `citations`, `documents` or `identity`. Invariants 1 to 5, 7 and 12 apply: tenant isolation as a hard boundary, authorization before protected content, no content in logs, audit or exceptions, and no cross-module private imports.

Step 14 (`grounded_generation`, frozen) turns untrusted source claims plus a question into a validated, cited, recorded answer. Step 15 (`conversations`, frozen) stores user messages and reference-only assistant turns (ADR-039 C1: the answer text is not stored and cannot be rebuilt from the content-free `GenerationRecord`).

## Problem
Join retrieval, grounded generation and the conversation log into one workflow, a question in and a validated, cited answer out, linked to its conversation, without (a) adding a dependency edge or an authorization shortcut, (b) duplicating the prompt, citation or output-validation pipeline, (c) returning anything that did not pass validation, (d) claiming that three separate transactions in three modules are one, (e) storing answer text, or (f) reopening any frozen decision.

## Decision
**D1. Neutral claim input in `grounded_generation.api` [Q1, OWNER APPROVED 2026-10-18].** `GenerateGroundedAnswerCommand.claims` needs `citations.api.SourceClaim`, built from `citations` id types that `answering` may not import. The narrowest compliant integration is strictly additive: `ClaimReference` (document id, version id, chunk id, ordinal, content hash; plain `UUID`, `int`, `str`, validated in `__post_init__`: UUID types, ordinal `0..1_000_000` and not a bool, 64 lowercase hex digest) and one function `claims_from_references(tuple[ClaimReference, ...]) -> tuple[SourceClaim, ...]`. The conversion lives inside `grounded_generation`, which already owns the `citations` edge. `answering` passes the function's result straight into the unchanged command, so it never names a `citations` type. A reference is untrusted and is **not** proof of authorization: every provenance, proof and lifecycle check of Step 14 still runs on the converted claims (tests: a forged hash passes the shape check and is refused by the chunk store). No existing class, signature, behaviour, test, template or hash changed. `api.lock` for `grounded_generation` gained 9 lines and lost none (verified by diff). The DAG, `citations` boundaries and the approved `grounded_answer` v1 template are unchanged.

**D2. One use case, one fixed order [application layer].** `answering.api.Answer` (`@requires("answering.answer_question", "documents.read_document")`, `@not_audited`) runs: (1) require a `TenantContext`; (2) validate the command (question non-blank, no NUL, at most `max_question_characters`; candidates per D3); (3) ask Access Control for ONE decision over exactly the candidates and require it to be allowed, for this tenant and principal, non-empty and no wider than asked; (4) `AppendUserMessage`; (5) `SearchIndex` with the granted scope and its proof; (6) refuse any hit outside the granted scope; (7) if no hits, return insufficient evidence without generating; (8) `GenerateGroundedAnswer` with `claims_from_references(hits)` and the configured `config_id`; (9) `AppendAssistantTurn` with the returned record id; (10) return. Authorization precedes the first write, so an unauthorized or disabled caller leaves no message behind. The user message precedes search, so the attempt is recorded even if later steps fail. The answer is built only from the validated `GroundedAnswer`; `answering` never sees provider text. Collaborators are one-method Protocol ports satisfied structurally by the public use cases of `access_control`, `retrieval`, `grounded_generation` and `conversations`.

**D3. Caller-supplied, bounded candidate documents [Q2, OWNER APPROVED].** The command carries a tuple of distinct document UUIDs: non-empty, at most `max_candidates` (default 100, ceiling 10 000 = Access Control's scope limit), exact types, duplicates refused (not merged). Every identifier is untrusted: Access Control decides the granted subset and only that subset is searched or sent anywhere. Denial, unknown principal, other tenant, deleted document and every inconsistent decision are one code, `answering_not_authorized`, with no reason, count or id. Searching all documents the caller may read is **not supported** (it needs a `documents` edge and a listing API; not added). `documents.read_document` and the kind `document` are literal copies pinned to `documents.api` by a test, as `citations` does.

**D4. Application use case, not an HTTP endpoint [Q3, OWNER APPROVED].** No framework, endpoint or route was added. The outer authentication/API boundary remains responsible for authenticating the caller and rejecting disabled principals, exactly as for `conversations` (ADR-039). Access Control denies a disabled principal as part of its decision (tested), but that is a consequence of its policy reader, not a substitute for the boundary's check, and `answering` does not claim to enforce authentication.

**D5. Partial-failure semantics [Q4, OWNER APPROVED].** Three modules, three transactions; nothing here is atomic across them.
1. *Before the user message is stored* (shape, authorization, conversation errors): nothing written; stable code returned.
2. *Search or generation fails after the user message is stored*: the immutable user message stays; the callee's stable code propagates unchanged. Retrying with the same `client_message_id` and text replays the message (`replayed=True`, no duplicate, no new audit fact); the same id with different text is `conversation_message_conflict`, raised before search.
3. *No usable claims*: generation is not called, no record exists, no assistant turn can be linked; the turn is `insufficient_evidence` and history holds only the user message.
4. *Generation succeeded but the link failed*: the answer is **withheld**; `answering_turn_not_recorded` is raised in the cause's category (conflict, infrastructure, otherwise application) with the cause's code as internal `detail`. The generation record and its `generation_recorded` audit fact exist and are **unlinked orphans**; no `assistant_turn_added` fact exists. The answer text is not recoverable.
5. *Retries do not give exactly-once generation*: the Step 14 contract has no idempotency key, so a retry (after a failed link, or a resubmission of a completed turn) makes another provider call and another record; after a completed turn a second assistant turn is appended. Concurrent identical requests store one user message and may produce two records. Not deduplicated, by design and by contract.
6. *Rejected or model-refused outputs* have a record and ARE linked (the history shows a turn happened); `statements` is empty and only the stable rejection code is returned.

**D6. Configuration [Q4.5].** `AnsweringSettings` (`LDA_ANSWERING__*`): `config_id` (required, no default, shape `rag-<64 lowercase hex>`), `max_candidates`, `max_question_characters` (default 2 000), `max_sources` (default 8). `build_answering` refuses, at start-up and with field names only, any limit a collaborator would reject later (`max_sources` above retrieval `max_results` or the effective grounded/citations `max_sources`; `max_question_characters` above retrieval `max_query_characters` or conversations `max_text_chars`). `config_id` is operator-supplied and validated for shape only. **It is not a computed fingerprint of the effective runtime configuration and nothing verifies that it matches the evaluated configuration; Step 17 must address binding it.**

**D7. Audit.** `Answer` is `@not_audited` with a reason: it emits no fact of its own. Each write is audited by the use case that makes it, all under the caller's `request_id`: `conversations.user_message_added`, `grounded_generation.generation_recorded`, `conversations.assistant_turn_added` (verified in PostgreSQL). A failed step produces no success fact for itself. **There is no single fact meaning "answer delivered"**, and a request that fails after generation leaves a recorded generation without an `assistant_turn_added` fact; reconciling these is operational work (see Operational implications). Replays add no fact (ADR-039 C6).

**D8. No streaming; other planned ADRs.** `Answer` returns only after validation and linking; there is no path that releases unvalidated provider text, partial text or a stream. ADR-033 ("No streaming of unvalidated answer text", Planned for step 16) is therefore satisfied in behaviour but **not authored as a separate ADR**; ADR-021 (API versioning, Planned for step 16) concerns an HTTP surface that D4 deliberately does not create. Both stay `Planned` in the decisions log and belong with the step that adds a delivery boundary.

**D9. Guards and governance.** Three existing architecture tests were amended, each additively and none weakened: `test_audit_boundaries.py` (the exact reviewed `@not_audited` set gains `Answer`, ADR-019 D9); `test_retrieval_boundaries.py` (the importer and dependant pins admit `answering`, public `retrieval.api` only, plus `bootstrap/answering.py`; the pins' own text anticipated a consumer); `test_citations_boundaries.py` (the importer set admits `bootstrap/answering.py`, which reads only the `citations` settings slice as `bootstrap/grounded_generation.py` does). `tests/architecture/test_answering_boundaries.py` pins the manifest edges, the unchanged DAG entry, public-API-only imports, the literal copies against their sources, and that the `grounded_generation` API only grew. `.importlinter` was regenerated through `archguard gen-importlinter` (reordered list entries plus the `answering` contracts). `CODEOWNERS` is unchanged.

## Rationale
Option B keeps the `citations` knowledge where it already lives and needs no new edge. Authorizing before the first write is the cheapest way to leave no trace for a caller who may not read anything they named. Returning only after the link succeeded makes success mean "the conversation reflects this turn". Withholding on a failed link is the only behaviour that never shows an answer the history cannot account for, given that answers are not stored.

## Alternatives considered
- **A: add a `citations` edge to `answering`.** Needs a DAG change, an amended importer pin and a larger reviewed surface for the same effect. Rejected by the owner (Q1).
- **C: a mapping adapter in `bootstrap/`.** Puts mapping code in a composition-only layer. Rejected.
- **Storing the answer text so a failed link or a lost response is recoverable.** Reopens ADR-039 C1 with retention, purge and provenance consequences. Not done.
- **Returning the answer with a "not recorded" flag when the link fails.** Shows an answer that history cannot account for and that cannot be shown again. Rejected (Q4.2).
- **Deduplicating a retry by reading the conversation for an existing assistant turn.** A heuristic that is not an idempotency guarantee and would make a lost answer permanently unobtainable. Not done; no idempotency key was added.
- **Append the user message first, authorize afterwards.** Leaves messages from callers who may read nothing they named. Rejected.

## Consequences
New module `answering` (no schema, no migration, no infrastructure layer); additive `ClaimReference` and `claims_from_references`; `bootstrap/answering.py`; one configuration slice. Callers must treat `answering_turn_not_recorded` as "generated but not recorded, answer lost".

## Security implications
Tenant and principal come only from the `TenantContext`; the decision's scope must match both and be no wider than asked; a hit outside the granted scope stops the turn (a search result is not a grant); Step 14 re-authorizes the cited documents three more times. No question, passage or answer text appears in a log, audit fact, error or repr (tested, including mutation checks). Prompt-injection defenses remain mitigations, not a proof. Grounding and citation validation do not establish legal correctness or completeness. A proof lives 60 seconds; a slow search fails closed and leaves the user message.

## Operational implications
Orphan generation records (failed link, retried turns) accumulate; they are content-free and immutable, and their retention is unresolved (below). Provider calls are not deduplicated, so retries cost. Per-attempt observability is the three audit facts per `request_id`; no new metric or log was added.

## Migration implications
None: no schema, no migration. Existing callers of `GenerateGroundedAnswerCommand` are unaffected.
